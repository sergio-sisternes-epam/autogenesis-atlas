## Context

After sequential discussion of Original Counter 1 (premature structure / maintenance tax), the user requested that the remaining original counters be captured as open points for the future rather than continuing one-at-a-time now.

## What happened

**Counter 1 status (substantially addressed)**  
- Agent (not human) maintains the graph.  
- Multiverse model: multiple co-existing realities/ideas allowed; “our reality is our own”.  
- Explicit pruning of old discussions or tokens.  
- Cost reframed as amortization: structuring today avoids discarded-session inference waste and future human re-thinking.  
Residual low: concrete pruning criteria, reality-marking convention, and navigability bounds still open but no longer high-severity blockers.

**Counters 2–5 recorded as open**

2. Graphs readily encode false, vague, or inconsistent knowledge—especially when content is provisional.  
   Risk of reifying forming ideas; later contraction becomes costly once edges exist.

3. Storage and navigation do not reliably produce insight or novel thesis.  
   Graphs optimise for storage more than generative insight; empirical pattern from linked-note systems is that insight still comes from conversation, not the structure itself.

4. Placement, cross-Atlas exploration, and ontological agreement create coordination and scope-creep costs.  
   “Best place” decisions and free exploration of other Atlases add navigation and agreement overhead.

5. Existing Autogenesis Discussion mode already solves the lightweight need.  
   Current mode (zero implement authority, no product writes, hard block on discussion→implement) already treats discussion as provisional and exploratory. Formal graph may over-solve.

## Outcome

Counters 2–5 are now durable open points in the Atlas, related to the discussion hub. They can be re-opened sequentially later or folded into a design plan when the discuss discipline is formalised. Counter 1 is treated as sufficiently answered for present purposes.
