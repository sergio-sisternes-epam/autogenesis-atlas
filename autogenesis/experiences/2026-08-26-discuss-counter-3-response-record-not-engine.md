## Context

Sequential discussion of Original Counter 3 (storage and navigation do not reliably produce insight or novel thesis).

## What happened

User response:

- The graph is a high-fidelity record and navigation aid.
- Its value is persistence and amortisation.
- The conversion between human and AI is what produces new ideas (as in this conversation).
- The discussion graph allows us to accelerate human brain connections.

This accepts the core distinction of Counter 3 and pins the purpose of the graph: it is not a generative engine. Insight and novel thesis arise in the live human–AI conversation. The graph persists that work, makes it navigable across sessions, amortises discarded-session cost, and thereby speeds the next cycle of human connection-making.

## Outcome

Counter 3 is substantially addressed. The original worry (claiming the graph itself sparks new ideas) is rejected. The intended role is now explicit: record + map + amortisation + accelerator of human connection, not autonomous thesis production.

Low residual (optional, design-time): how the graph is presented so it actually accelerates connections (surface the right branches at the right time) rather than merely accumulating records.
