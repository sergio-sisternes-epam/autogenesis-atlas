## What happened

**Original Counter 3**  
Storage and navigation do not reliably produce insight or novel thesis.

Linked-note and graph systems (Zettelkasten, Roam, Obsidian, etc.) are optimised for storage and retrieval. The hoped-for emergent insight (“the links will spark new ideas”) frequently fails to materialise. Users report graph views abandoned, high note counts with zero output, and the realisation that the generative work still happens in free conversation; the graph mostly records it after the fact. Over-structuring early ideation can also anchor thinking toward conventional, high-probability directions rather than divergent ones.

Under the agent-maintained multiverse + TVA model already adopted, the graph is intended as a navigational map for branching human thought. The counter still asks whether that map itself generates novel thesis, or whether insight continues to come from the conversation that the map merely supports.
