## Context

Sequential discussion of Original Counter 4 (placement, cross-Atlas exploration, and ontological agreement create coordination and scope-creep costs).

## What happened

User response:

- Life is an act of balance. We need to decide when it is worth doing this.
- Placement / cross-Atlas exploration should be an option in some cases, not a default.
- Example: when we are lost, having a “friend” to support us and give a different point of view helps — but we do not do this all the time.
- Personal analogue: liked doing this with Helena; concern that it may have overwhelmed her.

This rejects mandatory “best place + explore any relevant Atlas” as a ritual. Cross-Atlas consultation is situational help, gated by need (lost, need another view), and bounded so the supporting source is not overwhelmed.

## Outcome

Counter 4 is substantially addressed. Placement and cross-Atlas exploration become optional, need-triggered, and balanced rather than first-class on every discussion. Ontological agreement is no longer required up front; other Atlases are consulted as friends, not as authorities that must be reconciled.

Low residual (design-time): concrete trigger for “we are lost / we need another view”, and a bound so the agent does not overwhelm the consulted Atlas or the conversation.
