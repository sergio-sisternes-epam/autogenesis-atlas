## Context

After addressing Original Counter 1 (maintenance tax → agent-maintained multiverse + cost amortization) and Original Counter 2 (reification → TVA-style evaluation of branches), the remaining points are low-severity design residuals. User requested they be captured for the future rather than resolved now.

## What happened

**Residuals from Counter 1 (agent-maintained multiverse + amortization)**

- Concrete pruning criteria (what, when, and how the agent decides to prune old discussions or specific tokens).
- Reality-marking convention: clear, cheap way to distinguish “our current reality” from co-existing alternative realities so the map stays navigable.
- Navigability bounds so the multiverse does not itself become expensive to query or traverse.
- Implicit break-even expectation: the graph must actually be re-used in future sessions for the amortization claim to hold.

**Residuals from Counter 2 (TVA-style branching control)**

- Concrete criteria for TVA evaluation: what counts as “value against the original objective”.
- Cadence / trigger of evaluation (when does the agent run the keep / expand / terminate decision).
- How a “terminate” decision is recorded so edges do not dangle and the graph remains coherent.

## Outcome

All listed residuals are now durable open points in the Atlas. They are design-time items to be pinned when the discuss discipline (or the top-level skill) is formalised. They do not block further sequential discussion of the remaining original counters.
