User is formalising Autogenesis “discussion” as a top-level catalog skill that Autogenesis will import via the multi-harness substrate contract. We are reviewing the think-challenge counters one at a time. Rule in force: when a “One X at a time” happens, create new related pages in the Atlas.

## What happened

Counter 1 (highest severity under the new framing):

Making “discuss” a first-class catalog skill that Autogenesis imports turns a previously light *mode* into prescribed procedure.

Classic CSCW finding: people reject formalisms because of cognitive overhead, premature structure, and difficulty capturing tacit social practice. Formalising what culture already does well can be a step backward.

Agent-skill research: seemingly relevant skills frequently induce Excessive Procedure (mandatory verification, heavy pipelines, context overhead from skill body). A discuss skill whose core moves are “name clear subject → locate best Atlas place → grow nodes + typed edges → explore other Atlases” tends to convert every exploratory exchange into that prescribed work.

Key design tension stated: Can the skill stay as light as the current Discussion mode (zero implement authority, no product writes, conversation-first), or does elevating it to a reusable top-level skill with graph/Atlas discipline inevitably pull it toward the heavier formalisms that both humans and agents have historically resisted?

User invited to challenge, accept as constraint, steel-man a light version, or request deeper evidence.
