## Context

Discussion under autogenesis on creating a progressive patterns module. Location, admission bar, template, and scope were locked. Capability surface was proposed, then challenged with think-challenge before pinning.

## What was pinned for v1

- **Identify** — flag candidates only  
- **Extract** — draft only; admission still proven repeated use  
- **Create** — requires evidence of repeated use  
- **Record known-use** — cheap, living Known uses (ideally tied to remember/Exit)  
- **Load** — progressive by id/category  
- **List/query** — progressive  

## Explicitly deferred (do not forget)

- **Scan/detect** — deferred (false-candidate risk, mining precision problems)  
- **Relate** — deferred (pattern-language graph; not needed for one pattern)
