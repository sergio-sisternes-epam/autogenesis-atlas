## What happened

## What the user asked
The user requested “One question at a time” early in a formal Autogenesis discussion (mode: discussion, subject: autogenesis, path: design) about introducing a recipes module. Later they explicitly asked to capture the resulting facilitation pattern as memory and to propose follow-ups: (1) extract a pattern, (2) incorporate the pattern into Autogenesis discussion mode.

## What we did
Over a multi-turn conceptual design conversation we used a strict facilitation discipline:

- Exactly one open question per turn
- Every answer was immediately pinned as a decision
- Open requirements gaps were explicitly flagged as active risks and kept visible
- Proposals for procedures were offered, then confirmed or refined
- A living decision log was compiled on request
- The Card pattern (Activation Card) was respected throughout
- No implementation occurred (discussion mode)

This produced a coherent, fully pinned decision set for a complex new surface (recipes module) without scope drift or premature commitment.

## What we pinned about the pattern itself
- The high-level Autogenesis discussion/run separation and design-path rules are standard.
- The fine-grained “one question at a time + progressive micro-pinning + living decision log + explicit gap flagging” facilitation style is currently **ad-hoc** (emerged from user preference + good practice).
- It is valuable enough to consider as a candidate enhancement.

## Proposed follow-ups (explicitly requested by user)
1. Extract the facilitation style as a named pattern (or recipe / internal module).
2. Incorporate the pattern into the Autogenesis discussion mode (so future design discussions can opt into or default to this disciplined questioning style).
